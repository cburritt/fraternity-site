Our House on the Hill

Home to 50 brothers on the original Greek row at K-State, a 10-minute walk from campus.

Take a Look Inside

Built in 1938, our 8,064 square foot chapter house sits at 513 Sunset Ave in Manhattan. It has a full industrial kitchen, sleeping dorms and sleeping rooms, and two parking lots with 65 spaces combined.

Every room comes furnished with an XL twin bed, window treatments, a desk and a wardrobe per person.

Living In

Rent

$2,950 a semester or $575 monthly on the national standard 2 year lease. Utilities are included.

Meal Plan

$1,550 for meals from a personal chef. Lunch and dinner Monday through Friday, with chapter dinner on Wednesday.

Capacity

50 in-house members.

Location

On the original Greek row at K-State. A 10-minute walk or 4-minute drive to campus.
