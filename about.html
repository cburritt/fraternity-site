Our History

Pi Kappa Phi was founded on December 10, 1904 at the College of Charleston by Andrew Kroeg, Simon Fogarty, and Lawrence Mixson. The Delta Chi Chapter was chartered at Kansas State University in 1976 and has called Manhattan, Kansas home ever since.
